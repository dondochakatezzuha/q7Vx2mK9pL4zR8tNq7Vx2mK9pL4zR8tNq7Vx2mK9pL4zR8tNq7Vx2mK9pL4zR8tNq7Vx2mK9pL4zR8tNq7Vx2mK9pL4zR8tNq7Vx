Aro

Aro is the life essence that flows through every living being. Once Aro awakens, it determines the path of its user. The user guides Aro, but Aro is conscious and makes decisions through them.

ARO
Aro is a conscious life essence. Once awakened, an individual is assigned an Aro Aspect by their path. Each Aspect has its own abilities, and mastery can make different characters with the same Aspect develop in completely different ways.
Aro Growth
Every choice can strengthen or weaken Aro over time. Two characters with the same Aspect can awaken with completely different levels of Aro because of the choices they made. Aro reflects the person the character has become, not only the power they possess.
Aro Colours
Red means Aro is weakening. Green means Aro is growing stronger. Blue means Aro is close to awakening. Yellow means Aro is evolving. Once awakened, Blue can never appear again.
